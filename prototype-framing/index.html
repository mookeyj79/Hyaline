    Found while building this

    • Light glass reads as flat in every treatment. The port is 96% opaque, so even with the window moved over the horizon and the hiker, only a faint ghost shows. That is the port's opacity, not the framing. Framing cannot fix it without changing pixels.
    • Glass and a pasted backdrop disagree. In C the window shows the blurred forest but sits on a flat slate card. With Dark glass you can see it; with flat it is seamless.
    • Real compositor shadow is safe. picom's shadow left flat windows identical and changed glass windows by at most 2 of 255 levels, which you cannot see.
    • The frame is Adwaita from metacity. "No title bar" crops to the app's own client area (menu bar stays). It cannot apply to B and D, which show the real desktop around the whole window.
    • Wallpapers: Forest under gray sky (Dark), White Sands dunes (Light). Card colors come from the palette's backdrop role.
